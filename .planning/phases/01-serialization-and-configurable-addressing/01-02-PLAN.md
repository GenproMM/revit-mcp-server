<objective>
Harden the serialization seam that plan 01-01 built: bound the queue wait without shrinking a
call's own request budget, make every failure say honestly whether the mutation was sent, and
let the five confirmed read-only POSTs bypass the queue through a fail-safe allowlist.

Purpose: SER-03 (the lock is always released), SER-04 (queueing never shrinks a call's budget,
and an over-long wait says nothing was sent) and SER-02 (reads and `/status/` are not queued
behind mutations) — ROADMAP success criteria 2, 3 and 4, all proven at the unit tier.

Output: an extended `bridge.py` and `tests/unit/test_bridge_serialization.py`.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| tool coroutine → bridge lock | concurrent tool calls from FastMCP (one task per request) contend for the lock |
| bridge → pyRevit Routes | a sent request may be applied by Revit after the client gave up |
| bridge error string → model | the model decides whether to retry based on the wording alone |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-06 | Tampering | `_revit_call` failure wording | high | mitigate | Distinct "was not sent to Revit" (certain pre-send failures only) vs "outcome UNKNOWN … may have been applied … Verify" (all other transport errors); queue-busy says "NOT sent to Revit; nothing was sent"; all are `Error:` strings that `format_response` passes through unchanged (Tasks 1-2 tests) |
| T-01-07 | Denial of service | module-global lock | high | mitigate | `async with` only; `CancelledError` never caught; release tests after exception, post-send timeout, holder cancel, waiter cancel and bound expiry (Task 2) |
