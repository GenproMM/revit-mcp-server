<objective>
Upgrade the MCP SDK from 1.9.0 to 1.30.x, the first prerequisite of the phase: server-side elicitation
(the confirmation channel of D-05) does not exist in 1.9.0. Per D-24 the target is `mcp[cli]>=1.30,<2`,
not 2.x; per D-06 the bump is costly (it touches every tool registrar through the audit wrapper and the
fleet payload) and is gated by a human review of the resolved lock before anything is installed.

Purpose: SAFE-19. Everything else in Phase 7 needs `ctx.session.elicit_form`. The two code-level
consequences research measured are handled here: `structured_output=False` as the default in
`audit.install_audited_tools` (otherwise all 59 tools gain an outputSchema and call_tool changes shape),
and an in-memory elicitation harness shaped like the unchanged Hermes handler (D-05: accept arrives with
empty content; consent is the action only). The payload-side pywin32 shim is plan 07-03.

Output: the pin and lock, the audit default, `tests/unit/elicitation_support.py`,
`tests/unit/test_sdk_upgrade.py`; the full suite and cold start green on 1.30.
</objective>
