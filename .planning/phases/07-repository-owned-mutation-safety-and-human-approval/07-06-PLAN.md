<objective>
Make the base policy provably complete and its decisions provably those the user chose. A guard test derives
the true tool and route inventory from the source (the same AST discovery Phase 2 and Phase 5 use) and fails
on any tool or route without a classification, on a method or document-binding mismatch, and on any decision
that differs from a hand-written expectation table. Then implement the D-10 / D-22 checks as a pure
`policy.decide` and route the approval flow through it.

tool) and SAFE-07 (base defaults per D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-23, D-25). D-15 relaxations by an operator
remain possible by design. Facts arrive in 07-10/07-11; here `decide` is exercised with fact dictionaries.

Output: `tests/unit/test_policy_inventory.py`, `tests/unit/test_policy_decisions.py`, `policy.decide`,
corrections in `policy.base.toml`, and `approval.authorize` using `decide`.
</objective>
